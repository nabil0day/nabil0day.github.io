Journal TuSegNet: A Transformer-Based and Attention-Enhanced Architecture for Brain Tumor Segmentation

IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society Q1, 2025

TuSegNet is a hybrid CNN-transformer model with ASPP and channel attention for brain tumor segmentation. It achieves Dice scores of 0.895, 0.910, and 0.930 on three datasets, surpassing Swin UNet and TransUNet. Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of each module, showcasing strong performance and clinical relevance.
